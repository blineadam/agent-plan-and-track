# logsift

logsift is a command-line tool that stands as a testament to what a small, focused utility can achieve. Born from a late-night debugging session, it has grown into a vibrant tapestry of ideas about how engineers should read their logs.

logsift is fast, flexible, and reliable. It seamlessly integrates with your shell, your editor, and your CI pipeline, so you can delve into a failing run without ever leaving the terminal.

> "The crucial thing about a log tool is that you stop noticing it — until the day you need it." (Priya Raman, SRE lead)

Boasting a breathtaking range of features, logsift showcases a pivotal approach to log parsing. It reads plain text, JSON lines, and gzip archives, groups repeated errors, and prints a short summary at the bottom.

Installation is a single step — download the binary and put it on your PATH. Building from source works with Go 1.20–1.22, and the test suite runs in under ten seconds.

The future looks bright for logsift. Whether you are a seasoned SRE or just starting out, it will enhance your workflow and make every incident a little less painful.
