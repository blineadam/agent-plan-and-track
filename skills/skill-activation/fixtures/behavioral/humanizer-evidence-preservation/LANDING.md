# Shipwright

Most deploy tools get one thing wrong. Here's what it is.

Whether you're a solo developer or a growing team, shipping code shouldn't be scary. Say goodbye to flaky deploys; say hello to confidence. That's where Shipwright comes in. It's more than just a CI tool; it's a seamless way to leverage your existing pipeline. The result? Faster shipping.

Teams love it. Run `npx shipwright deploy --env prod` and your build goes live in under 90 seconds. Rollbacks happen automatically when you have health checks configured; without them, you roll back by hand with `npx shipwright rollback`.

Docs live at https://example.com/shipwright/docs.
